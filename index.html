What we make

Whether it’s an intimate home installation, a sweeping event backdrop, or a seasonal wreath for your door, every piece is made to last.

  • Dried Bouquets

    Carefully composed arrangements of preserved botanicals — strawflowers, pampas, lunaria, and grasses — built to last a lifetime.

  • Shop Activations

    Statement installations and seasonal styling for retail spaces, pop-ups, and boutique storefronts that want to make an impression.

  • Home Décor

    Custom dried arrangements for interior spaces — bespoke to your palette, room, and aesthetic. Lasting beauty without maintenance.

  • Seasonal Wreaths

    Hand-foraged and assembled wreaths for every season — earthy autumn tones, soft winter whites, and everything in between.
